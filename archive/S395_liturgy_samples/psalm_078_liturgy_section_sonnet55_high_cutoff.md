## Modern Jewish Liturgical Use (Psalm 78)

*Texts searched (Sefaria): the Ashkenaz, Sefard (Hasidic) and Edot HaMizrach siddurim; the Chabad weekday siddur; the Rosh Hashanah and Yom Kippur machzorim of all three rites; the full selichot (Ashkenaz, Lithuanian and Polish; Edot HaMizrach); the Ashkenaz kinnot and the Edot HaMizrach Tisha B'Av order; two Haggadot; Ma'avar Yabbok, Seder Ma'amadot, Tikkun HaKlali, Perek Shirah, Yizkor, Akdamut, Keter Malkhut and the Azharot. Not searched: festival machzorim for Pesach, Shavuot and Sukkot beyond what the siddurim carry, and the Yemenite, Italian, Romaniote and other rites. A place missing below may still exist: nothing here shows that a verse is said ONLY somewhere, or in 'certain' services.*

### v. 2: אֶפְתְּחָה בְמָשָׁל פִּי, אַבִּיעָה חִידוֹת מִנִּי־קֶדֶם

**Where:** The Prayer for Dew (Tal), inserted into the first blessing of the Amidah (the Pesach Musaf, when Tal is said). It appears in the Ashkenaz siddur (Festivals) and the Sefard (Hasidic) siddur (Holidays).

**The text:** Sefard, between the Avot blessing and "Magen Avraham":
מֶלֶךְ עוֹזֵר וּמוֹשִׁיעַ וּמָגֵן: בְּדַעְתּוֹ. ⟦אַבִּיעָה חִידוֹת⟧. בְּעַם זוּ בְּזוּ בְּטַל לְהַחֲדוֹת. טַל גֵיא וּדְשָׁאֶיהָ לַחֲדוֹת
"King, helper, saviour and shield. In His knowledge, *I will utter riddles*… with dew to gladden; dew of the valley and its grasses, to gladden." The Ashkenaz text opens with the rubric "open the Ark."

**Note:** The poet takes only the two words "avi'ah chidot" from the psalm's opening, reusing its "I will utter riddles" as the opening of his own poem. The psalm's narrative plays no part.

### v. 12: נֶגֶד אֲבוֹתָם עָשָׂה פֶלֶא, בְּאֶרֶץ מִצְרַיִם שְׂדֵה־צֹעַן

**Where:** Motzaei Shabbat. The whole verse appears in the Sefard siddur's Melava Malka zemirot and in the Edot HaMizrach Havdalah section, after Veyiten Lecha.

**The text:** In both books it sits in a run of verses that all begin with nun:
נָהָר פְּלָגָיו יְשַׂמְּחוּ עִיר אֱלֹהִים. קְדֹשׁ מִשְׁכְּנֵי עֶלְיוֹן: ⟦נֶגֶד אֲבוֹתָם עָשָׂה פֶלֶא. בְּאֶרֶץ מִצְרַיִם שְׂדֵה צֹעַן⟧: נָחִיתָ כַצֹּאן עַמֶּךָ. בְּיַד מֹשֶׁה וְאַהֲרֹן
"A river whose streams gladden the city of God… *Before their fathers He wrought wonders in the land of Egypt, the field of Zoan*. You led Your people like a flock, by the hand of Moses and Aaron."

**Note:** The neighbouring verses are Numbers 32:32, Deut 18:15, Ps 46:5, Ps 77:21, Prov 7:17, Prov 20:27 and Song 4:11. The verse therefore seems to stand there by its initial letter, not by its sense.

### v. 23: וַיְצַו שְׁחָקִים מִמָּעַל וְדַלְתֵי שָׁמַיִם פָּתָח

See the echoes below. The Neilah and Hoshana Rabba refrain is at most a loose allusion to this verse.

### v. 38: וְהוּא רַחוּם יְכַפֵּר עָוֺן

**Where:** The whole verse is among the most widely printed in the liturgy, and appears in these settings:
- **(a) Uva LeTzion / Ve'atah Kadosh (Kedushah deSidra).**
  - Ashkenaz: weekday Shacharit and Shabbat Mincha.
  - Sefard: weekday Shacharit (Ashrei–Uva LeTzion), Rosh Chodesh and Shabbat Mincha.
  - Chabad: weekday Shacharit.
  - Edot HaMizrach: weekday Shacharit, Rosh Hodesh and Shabbat Mincha.
  - Edot HaMizrach Tisha B'Av, night and day.
  - The same Ashrei–Uva LeTzion block is printed in the Mincha and Neilah sections of the Rosh Hashanah and Yom Kippur machzorim of all three rites.
  - On Motzaei Shabbat it recurs in Ve'atah Kadosh at Maariv: Ashkenaz (Viyehi Noam), Sefard, Chabad, and Edot HaMizrach (Arvit, after the Amidah).
- **(b) Pesukei Dezimra, among the verses after Hodu** (the Hodu itself is 1 Chronicles 16). Weekday and Shabbat Shacharit in Ashkenaz, Sefard, Chabad and Edot HaMizrach. It is also in the Rosh Hashanah and Yom Kippur machzorim of all three rites.
- **(c) Pesukei Dezimra, at the end of Yehi Chevod.** The same siddurim, machzorim and rites as (b).
- **(d) Opening verses of Maariv, just before Barchu ("Vehu Rachum").**
  - Weekday Maariv: Ashkenaz, Sefard, Chabad (where it opens the section) and Edot HaMizrach.
  - The Maariv following Yom Kippur: Ashkenaz and Sefard.
  - The Arvit after Rosh Hashanah and the Tisha B'Av Arvit: Edot HaMizrach.
- **(e) Tachanun on Mondays and Thursdays ("Vehu Rachum").** The rubric positively assigns it to those days. Ashkenaz, Sefard and Chabad.
- **(f) The Edot HaMizrach Vidui series** (Hashivenu… Vehu Rachum… Atah Hashem lo tichla… Hoshi'enu). It is in weekday Shacharit and in the services for the Seventeenth of Tammuz, the Fast of Gedalya, the Tenth of Tevet and the Fast of Esther. The Seventeenth of Tammuz section also has a second, partial match earlier.
- **(g) The Erev Yom Kippur lashes (malkot).** Sefard machzor (after Mincha, as the section is labelled) and Edot HaMizrach machzor ("Lashes").

**The text:**
- (a) Ashkenaz weekday Shacharit:
  שָׁמְרָה זֹאת לְעֹלָם לְיֵצֶר מַחְשְׁבוֹת לְבַב עַמֶּךָ וְהָכֵן לְבָבָם אֵלֶיךָ: ⟦וְהוּא רַחוּם יְכַפֵּר עָוֹן וְלֹא יַשְׁחִית וְהִרְבָּה לְהָשִׁיב אַפּוֹ וְלֹא יָעִיר כָּל חֲמָתוֹ⟧: כִּי אַתָּה אֲדֹנָי טוֹב וְסַלָּח
  "Keep this forever, as the inclination of the thoughts of Your people's heart, and direct their hearts to You. *He, being merciful, forgives iniquity and does not destroy; He turns His anger back again and again, and does not rouse all His wrath.* For You, Lord, are good and forgiving."
- (b) Ashkenaz weekday:
  רוֹמְמוּ יְהֹוָה אֱלֹהֵֽינוּ וְהִשְׁתַּחֲווּ לְהַר קָדְשׁוֹ כִּי קָדוֹשׁ יְהֹוָה אֱלֹהֵֽינוּ: ⟦וְהוּא רַחוּם… כָּל־חֲמָתוֹ⟧: אַתָּה יְהֹוָה לֹא־תִכְלָא רַחֲמֶֽיךָ מִמֶּֽנִּי
  "Exalt the Lord our God and bow at His holy mountain, for the Lord our God is holy. *[v. 38]*. You, Lord, will not withhold Your mercy from me."
- (c) Ashkenaz weekday:
  כִּי לֹא יִטּשׁ יְהֹוָה עַמּוֹ וְנַחֲלָתוֹ לֹא יַעֲזֹב: ⟦וְהוּא רַחוּם… כָּל־חֲמָתוֹ⟧: יְהֹוָה הוֹשִׁיעָה הַמֶּֽלֶךְ יַעֲנֵֽנוּ בְיוֹם־קָרְאֵֽנוּ
  "For the Lord will not forsake His people, nor abandon His inheritance. *[v. 38]*.
