### P12 (flagged)

**Before:**

The difference between noise and word is old wisdom about thunder. In the Bṛhadāraṇyaka Upaniṣad, Prajāpati gives gods, humans and demons a single syllable, "Da," and each group hears a different command: the gods hear "Be subdued," humans "Give," demons "Be compassionate." The text adds that the thunder repeats it still: Da, Da, Da. The rabbis read Psalm 29:4 the same way. On קוֹל ה׳ בַּכֹּחַ, "the voice of the LORD in power," Rashi says that at Sinai God צִמְצֵם אֶת קוֹלוֹ לְפִי כֹּחָן שֶׁל יִשְׂרָאֵל, "narrowed His voice according to the strength of Israel," so that each hearer received it according to his capacity. The psalm goes the other way from both. The voice speaks seven times in many forms, and the answer is one word from everyone at once. Thunder is plural and has no words; worship is single and articulate.
