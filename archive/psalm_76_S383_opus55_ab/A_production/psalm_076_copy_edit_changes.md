## Changes
*For exact before/after text, see [psalm_076_copy_edit_diff.md](psalm_076_copy_edit_diff.md).*

1. [7, 9e] **Introduction**: Softened "מְעוֹנָתוֹ is a den — specifically … the den of a big cat" to "can be a den … in several biblical passages, specifically the den of a big cat," and likewise made the bestiary claim non-absolute.  
Why: The Hebrew noun מְעוֹנָה/מָעוֹן is broader than "big-cat den" in biblical usage, so the original wording overstated the lexical evidence.

2. [2, 9b, 10] **Introduction**: Recast the structural summary from "two panels of roughly equal weight, each closed by סלה" to "four movements, punctuated by two סלה pauses," and glossed "superscription" as "the psalm's heading."  
Why: The original description contradicted its own four-part breakdown and misdescribed the function of the two Selah markers; "superscription" also needed a brief gloss for non-specialists.

3. [9b] **Introduction**: Changed "The battle is over and unrepeatable" to "The battle is over as the poem tells it."  
Why: The perfect forms support completed portrayal, not the stronger claim that the event is inherently unrepeatable.

4. [7] **Introduction**: Corrected the Jeremiah example to דִּין־יָתוֹם לֹא־דָנוּ, with an accurate English gloss.  
Why: The original phrasing blurred the Hebrew syntax and mistranslated the action as "they do not plead" rather than "they did not judge."

5. [7, 9b, 10] **Introduction**: Replaced "Four times the same sound closes the line" with "Four times the same sound recurs in the psalm's closing movement," and glossed "Elohistic hymn."  
Why: The fear-root terms do recur, but not as line-end closers in the way stated; "Elohistic" also needed explanation for a general reader.

6. [9b] **Liturgical — Full psalm**: Changed "The choice is more than a pun" to "The choice may be more than a pun."  
Why: The broader liturgical rationale is plausible but not demonstrated as fact in the paragraph, so the sentence needed an honest hedge.

7. [7] **Verse 1**: Changed "shares this heading word for word" to "shares all but the Asaphite attribution."  
Why: Psalm 67 does not match the heading exactly, since Psalm 76 includes לְאָסָף and Psalm 67 does not.

8. [5] **Verse 4**: Restored Hebrew Unicode in the Job citation, changing "yagbihu uf" to יַגְבִּיהוּ עוּף.  
Why: Running Hebrew quotations must appear in Hebrew script rather than transliteration.

9. [10, 7] **Verse 5**: Glossed "piyyutim" as "liturgical poems," corrected "anaphora" to "epistrophe (the same word hammering at the end of line after line)," and changed "seven centuries apart" to "more than a millennium apart."  
Why: The repeated word comes at line ends, not line beginnings, and the chronological distance between the psalm and Yannai is far greater than seven centuries.

10. [7, 9h] **Verse 6**: Replaced the inaccurate Greek citation "ἀσύνετος καρδία" with the plain gloss "foolish of heart," and changed "the Bible's great lament over Assyria" to "Nahum's dirge over Assyria."  
Why: The Greek wording quoted was not the form used in the verse, and "great" was an unsupported ranking claim.

11. [10, 7, 9h] **Verse 7**: Glossed "Chaoskampf" as "the mythic combat with the sea," removed the incorrect claim that biblical Hebrew normally says "סוּס וָרֶכֶב," and changed "the most cosmic sentence in the psalm" to "this cosmic sentence in the psalm."  
Why: The special term needed explanation, the word-order claim was contradicted by biblical usage, and "most" was an unsupported internal ranking.

12. [7, 10] **Verse 9**: Reworked the sound-pattern claim from "same consonant cluster, š-m-m" to "repeated š/m sounds," and replaced "strophe" with "section."  
Why: שָׁמָּה and מִשָּׁמַיִם do echo phonetically, but they do not share the exact consonant cluster claimed; "strophe" also added unnecessary jargon.

13. [9h, 10] **Verse 11**: Changed "The hardest line in the psalm" to "A difficult line in the psalm" and glossed BDB as "the standard Hebrew lexicon BDB."  
Why: "Hardest" was an unsupported superlative, and the lexicon acronym needed a brief identification.

14. [CITATION FIX] [7] **Verse 11**: Corrected the Daniel 3:28 quotation from יְהֵא שְׁמֵיהּ דֶּאֱלָהָא מְבָרַךְ to בְּרִיךְ אֱלָהֲהוֹן דִּי־שַׁדְרַךְ מֵישַׁךְ וַעֲבֵד נְגוֹ.  
Why: The original Aramaic line does not occur in Dan 3:28; the replacement is an actual contiguous substring of the cited verse.

15. [10] **Verse 12**: Replaced "nearly a hapax" with "very rare: three occurrences in the Bible," and glossed "theophany" as "appearance of God."  
Why: "Hapax" and "theophany" are technical terms that were not needed without explanation.

16. [CITATION FIX] [7] **Verse 12**: Corrected the Isaiah 18:7 fragment from "מֵעַם נוֹרָא" to "וּמֵעַם נוֹרָא."  
Why: The quoted fragment needed to match the actual verse as a contiguous substring; the prefixed וּ is part of the biblical text.

17. [7] **Verse 12**: Changed "the same syllable closing three lines" to "the same syllable recurring across three lines."  
Why: The cited fear-words do recur, but not as line-final syllables in the way the original sentence claimed.

18. [2, 9c] **Verse 13**: Changed "confirmed by a medieval liturgist" to "picked up by a medieval liturgist."  
Why: The paragraph rightly marked the vineyard-sense in the psalm as conjectural; "confirmed" improperly hardened that conjecture into proof.

19. [7, 9h] **Verse 13**: Removed the lexical overstatement "kings by birth" and changed "one of the great Persian meditations on fallen power" to "a Persian meditation on fallen power."  
Why: "By birth" is not part of the lexical contrast the verse establishes, and "one of the great" was an unsupported fame/ranking claim.
