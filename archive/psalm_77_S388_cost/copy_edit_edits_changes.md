## Changes
*For exact before/after text, see [psalm_077_copy_edit_diff.md](psalm_077_copy_edit_diff.md).*


1. [FACT-CHECK] [7,9(h)] **Introduction**: Revised the Cowper opening to describe a severe depressive relapse and removed the unsupported “best-known” ranking. The original overstated Cowper’s condition at the time of composition and made a reception claim the guide does not establish.
2. [FACT-CHECK] [7] **A Night Without Morning**: Corrected הָמָה from “the growl of lions” to “the growl of bears.” The biblical comparison for this root is to bears; the lion parallel uses a different verb.
3. [FACT-CHECK] [7] **The Same Memory, Turned**: Corrected the summary of Rashi and the Greek translators on v. 11. The original conflated unlike readings, misrepresented Rashi as sheer despair, and gave an English rendering that does not match the Greek.
4. [FACT-CHECK] [7] **The Same Memory, Turned**: Corrected the count of אָשִׂיחָה from its fourth appearance to its third. In Psalm 77 this form appears in vv. 4, 7, and 13.
5. [FACT-CHECK] [7] **Footprints in the Water**: Corrected the placement of אָנוּ לְיָהּ וְעֵינֵינוּ לְיָהּ within the Ne'ilah lament. The line belongs to the lament’s opening stanza, not its ending.
6. [FACT-CHECK] [7] **Liturgical — Full psalm**: Corrected the statement about fixed recitation. The whole psalm is not absent from all fixed settings; the source attests recitation with Parshat HaChodesh and on specified days of Sukkot.
7. [FACT-CHECK] [7,9(h)] **Liturgical — Full psalm**: Changed “closest emotional relative” to “a close emotional relative” and corrected “sits beside it” to “appears with it.” The original made an unsupported ranking claim and misstated the order within the Tikkun HaKlali.
8. [9(h)] **Liturgical — Full psalm**: Removed the unsupported ranking “Few psalms are so full of night.” The point about the psalm’s repeated nighttime language stands without a comparative claim the guide does not show.
9. [FACT-CHECK] [7] **Verse 1**: Limited the Hannah parallel to Meiri. Both Radak and Meiri equate עַל with לְ here, but only Meiri cites Hannah’s wording.
10. [2] **Verse 2**: Corrected the location of the crucial verb from “the first half” to “the second half.” The paragraph immediately discusses וְהַאֲזִין, which appears in the second half of the verse, so the original phrasing contradicted its own analysis.
11. [FACT-CHECK] [7] **Verse 2**: Changed “it ends” to “that passage ends” for Psalm 55. The report of being heard ends the cited passage in Ps 55:17–18, not the whole psalm.
12. [FACT-CHECK] [7] **Verse 3**: Corrected the summary of Ibn Ezra’s alternatives. The original misattributed the melted-strength reading to Ibn Ezra and omitted two readings he records.
13. [FACT-CHECK] [7] **Verse 3**: Corrected Malbim’s image so the sore belongs to the speaker, not to God’s hand. Malbim says the speaker’s oozing hand suggests that God’s supporting hand has weakened.
14. [8,10] **Verse 4**: Recast two technical grammatical labels into plain English. The stem name and the unexplained “cohortative” label added jargon without carrying the paragraph’s interpretive point.
15. [FACT-CHECK] [7] **Verse 5**: Changed “the verb belongs to kings disturbed by dreams” to a narrower claim. The verb is also used of Samson, so it is not restricted to dream-troubled kings.
16. [CITATION FIX] [FACT-CHECK] [7] **Verse 7**: Corrected the summary of Ibn Ezra’s two alternatives and fixed the Psalm 57 quotation to a contiguous fragment actually found in the verse. The original merged Ibn Ezra’s readings into one and quoted הַכָּבוֹד, which is not the biblical wording there.
17. [2] **Verse 7**: Removed the self-contradictory claim that “the translation here” says “their jibes.” The translation given above the paragraph says “my song,” so the original sentence contradicted the text on the page.
18. [FACT-CHECK] [7] **Verse 10**: Corrected the spelling claim about אַף. The shared element is the underlying אַף; the verse’s בְּאַף includes a prefixed preposition and is not “spelled exactly like” the particle.
19. [FACT-CHECK] [7] **Verse 10**: Corrected the Italian quotation from Boito’s *Otello*. The original mis-capitalized *dio*, misplaced accents, and added punctuation not present in the verified wording.
20. [FACT-CHECK] [7,8,10] **Verse 12**: Corrected the grammatical claim and replaced the unexplained term “cohortative” with plain English. Only the second “I will remember” form has the cohortative ending.
21. [FACT-CHECK] [7] **Verse 13**: Corrected the roles of Malbim and the Alshich in distinguishing the verse’s terms. Malbim distinguishes kinds of deeds; the Alshich distinguishes the two meditation verbs.
22. [FACT-CHECK] [7] **Verse 14**: Corrected the opening characterization of the commentators. Rashi does not identify a holy place here; he explains holiness as a divine mode of action.
23. [FACT-CHECK] [7] **Verse 16**: Corrected the scope of “Joseph” in the Asaph psalms. In Ps 81 it names the people brought out of Egypt, not specifically the northern tribes.
24. [5] **Verse 17**: Corrected the pointing of מַיִם in a running Hebrew quotation. The original form included an erroneous dagesh in the mem.
25. [FACT-CHECK] [7] **Verse 17**: Corrected the Racine comparison from water seeing to sky seeing. In the quoted passage it is heaven that sees the monster, while the wave recoils.
26. [FACT-CHECK] [7] **Verse 18**: Changed “hailstones” to “stones.” Radak and Meiri speak of stones or gravel here; the specific claim about hailstones overstates the evidence.
27. [FACT-CHECK] [7] **Verse 18**: Corrected the Maya creation comparison. The verified tradition has Huracan and Gucumatz speaking the world into being over the waters, not a thunderbolt directly making it.
28. [3,8,10] **Verse 18**: Removed an unsupported stem-based claim and the unexplained term “hitpael.” The stately effect comes from the verb’s sense in context, not from naming its stem.
29. [FACT-CHECK] [7] **Verse 19**: Corrected the summary of Meiri and the gloss on גַּלְגַּל. The original promoted Meiri’s secondary chariot-wheel reading to his main one and misdescribed Ps 83:14’s גלגל as the whirlwind itself.
30. [9(h)] **Verse 20**: Scaled down an unsupported superlative about the Sea crossing. The argument only needs the Sea as a paradigmatic rescue, not a claim that it was Israel’s greatest ever.
31. [FACT-CHECK] [7] **Verse 21**: Corrected the description of Rashi’s storm commentary. He comments on each storm verse, not on “almost every word.”
32. [FACT-CHECK] [7] **Verse 21**: Corrected the description of Ps 78:2. The phrase אַבִּיעָה חִידוֹת מִנִּי־קֶדֶם occurs early in the psalm, but it is not the opening words.
