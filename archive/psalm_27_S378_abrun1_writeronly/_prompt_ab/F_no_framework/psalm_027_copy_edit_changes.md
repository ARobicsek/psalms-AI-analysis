## Changes
*For exact before/after text, see [psalm_027_copy_edit_diff.md](psalm_027_copy_edit_diff.md).*

1. [7,10] **Light Is a Face**: Replaced “the flat predication here” with “the flat statement here.”  
   The original used a technical rhetorical term without explanation; the point did not require jargon.

2. [9(b)] **Light Is a Face**: Changed “it has already said ‘the LORD’s face is turned toward me’” to “it has already implied…”  
   The original stated as certainty what the evidence supports as an inference from the biblical idiom of shining face/light.

3. [9(e)] **Liturgical — Full psalm**: Changed “Adopted by the Baal Shem Tov’s circle and then by everyone” to “and then far more widely.”  
   “By everyone” overclaimed the scope; the evidence supports broad adoption, not universal uptake.

4. [10] **Liturgical — Key verses and phrases**: Added a gloss for *piyyut* as “liturgical poem.”  
   This is a technical liturgical term that an educated general reader may not confidently know on first encounter.

5. [7] **Verse 1**: Reworked the sound-play claim between אוֹרִי and אִירָא from “the same three sounds… differently arranged” to a simpler claim of phonetic echo.  
   The original letter/sound analysis was inaccurate; the words do echo, but they are not built from the same three sounds rearranged.

6. [7,9(b)] **Verse 1**: Revised the Septuagint note from “a verbal noun, the act of lighting rather than the light itself” to “my illumination… rather than the bare predicate ‘my light.’”  
   The original overstated the Greek semantics; φωτισμός can denote illumination without requiring the narrower sense “act of lighting.”

7. [7,9(e)] **Verse 3**: Changed “it always does the same thing” to “here it makes…” in the discussion of תַּחֲנֶה / מַחֲנֶה.  
   The original made an unnecessarily totalizing claim about the effect of the figure across Hebrew poetry.

8. [7] **Verse 3**: Corrected “takes a feminine verb, as in Genesis 32:9” to “takes feminine agreement, as in Genesis 32:9.”  
   Genesis 32:9 does not give a feminine verb with מַחֲנֶה there; the feminine agreement is seen more generally.

9. [10] **Verse 3**: Replaced “A demonstrative that points forward” with “A forward-pointing ‘this’.”  
   The original used grammatical terminology without explanation where plain language would do.

10. [7] **Verse 4**: Changed “Three infinitives name three distinct activities” to “The verse names three distinct activities.”  
   The original grammatical description was too specific and potentially inaccurate for שִׁבְתִּי in this context.

11. [9(e),7] **Verse 4**: Changed “In Jewish practice the difference has been quietly erased” to “In some Jewish practice the difference has been quietly softened.”  
   The original generalized too broadly about Jewish practice and overstated the liturgical convergence.

12. [7] **Verses 5–6**: Corrected the English translation of Rashi’s Joash reference to clarify that Jehosheba is Ahaziah’s sister.  
   As written, the translation misleadingly suggested that Jehosheba was Joash’s sister, which is false.

13. [8] **Verses 5–6**: Replaced “MT’s יָרוּם is imperfect” with “MT’s יָרוּם is a future form.”  
   The grammatical label added jargon without interpretive gain; the interpretive point is simply that the Hebrew is still future/open.

14. [10] **Verse 7**: Replaced “two words in apposition” with “two phrases set side by side with no connective.”  
   “Apposition” is a technical term that needed either explanation or replacement.

15. [10] **Verse 7**: Replaced “The catena is built…” with “The chain is built…”  
   “Catena” is technical and unnecessary here; the analysis is clear in plain language.

16. [7,9(c)] **Verse 8**: Changed “Its native habitat is Hosea” to “One striking parallel lies in Hosea,” and softened the conclusion to “That may explain the sequence here.”  
   The original overstated the Hosea connection; the shared language supports a relevant parallel, not a uniquely originating context.

17. [6,9(c)] **Verse 8**: Changed “The idiom has an Akkadian ancestor” to “The idiom also has an Akkadian parallel.”  
   “Ancestor” claimed a historical relationship stronger than the evidence provided; “parallel” states the comparison more responsibly.

18. [7,9(b)] **Verse 9**: Changed “Psalm 27 speaks before anything has happened” to “before the hiding has happened,” and softened “the whole difference between it and a lament.”  
   The psalm clearly includes existing troubles; the point is specifically that the hidden face is still being averted, not that nothing at all has occurred.

19. [7,8,9(b)] **Verses 11–12**: Removed the claim that שׁוֹרְרַי and “my singers” are spelled identically.  
   The proposed wordplay depended on an inaccurate form comparison and did not convincingly illuminate the verse.

20. [7,10,9(c)] **Verses 11–12**: Replaced “hapax” with “a word that appears only here” and hedged the Ugaritic-witness proposal (“may explain,” “on this reading would also mean…”).  
   “Hapax” was unexplained jargon, and the etymological argument was presented too confidently for a conjectural proposal.

21. [10] **Verses 11–12**: Replaced “the parallelism is decisive” with “the balancing of the two halves is decisive.”  
   This kept the interpretive point while avoiding an unexplained technical label.

22. [7] **Verses 11–12**: Changed “word for word, the thing verse 9 begs God not to do” to “the very thing verse 9 begs God not to do.”  
   The original was too exact: Psalm 71:11 and Psalm 27:9 share the core idea and root, but not the same wording.

23. [10] **Verse 13**: Replaced “the apodosis” with “the answering clause.”  
   “Apodosis” is a technical term that needed glossing or replacement; plain language carries the point.

24. [9(e)] **Verse 13**: Changed “Such marks generally flag…” to “Such marks often flag…”  
   The original overstated the uniform meaning of these Masoretic dots; “often” better matches the evidence.

25. [7] **Verse 14**: Replaced “Joshua’s charge, five times over” with “Joshua-language, echoing the repeated חֲזַק וֶאֱמָץ…”  
   The original count was inaccurate; the wording now preserves the observation without a false total.

26. [7] **Verse 14**: Corrected the backtick transliteration from `qa-**ŪH**` / `wə-qa-**ŪH**` to `qa-**VĒH**` / `wə-qa-**VĒH**`.  
   The original transliteration did not match the Hebrew pronunciation/form of קַוֵּה.
