**Governing idea**

The essay reads Psalm 76 as a reversal in which an army loses the power to act, while human action returns in vows and tribute.

**What it says the poem does and evokes**

It follows the song from God’s dwelling in Zion through broken weapons and stunned soldiers to a heavenly verdict for “all the lowly of the earth.” Its images make that deliverance both protective and frightening: Zion may be a shelter, but it may also be a hunter’s covert.

**Strongest insights**

- [poetic craft] “could not find their hands” — the essay lets the Hebrew image convey helplessness more strangely than “could not lift a hand.”
- [beyond the Bible] “what looks from outside like a cage may, from the singer’s position, be the dangerous resident’s covert” — Sennacherib’s boast becomes a way to see the difference between two views of Zion.
- [Bible] “Psalm 76 begins at the sanctuary and tells of victory there” — this draws out the essay’s contrast with the Song of the Sea.
- [poetic craft] “Its second סֶלָה ... falls after the lowly have been named” — the pauses connect the broken weapons with those the judgment protects.
- [Bible] “There is no ‘we,’ ‘us,’ or ‘our’ anywhere in the psalm” — a small observation that supports the essay’s attention to whom the song names, and when.

**Best writing**

“The psalm never shows an Israelite fighter taking those weapons away. It asks its hearers to imagine an army losing the ability to act.”

**Weak spots**

“In ordinary life, a judge’s sentence does something as well as saying something” briefly moves away from the poem to explain an analogy the surrounding lines already suggest. “If you have watched people with power decide what will happen to people without it” shifts abruptly from close reading to direct address.

**Claims worth checking**

The account of Rashi allowing both restraint of remaining rage and God girding himself with it needs checking against his commentary; the essay does not quote the relevant passage. Hosea 2:20 is Hebrew verse numbering; readers using many English Bibles will find the cited line at Hosea 2:18.
