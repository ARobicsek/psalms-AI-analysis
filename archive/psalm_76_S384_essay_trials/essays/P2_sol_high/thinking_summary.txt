**Connecting scripture themes**

Hosea 2:20 presents an inversion of breaking and sleeping, which could be woven into a cohesive point about security. I can connect this with Psalms 76, where it states, "Hosea's Judah shall be saved... not by bow, swords, or horses." This emphasizes that Hosea 2:20 signifies the breaking of weapons and war, indicating that Israel can sleep securely while their aggressors also find rest. It's fascinating how these themes intertwine!
