=== S5 xhigh: 5 new phrase targets ===
  v1: מִזְמוֹר...שִׁיר
      Double genre-label 'psalm'+'song' co-occurs ~9x in Psalter (30,48,65-68,75,76,83,87,108); possible composite/intensified liturgical genre marker, not mere redundancy.
  v1: πρός τὸν Ἀσσύριον (LXX)
      Absent from MT entirely. Later interpretive gloss tying psalm to Sennacherib narrative (2 Kgs 19//Isa 37); demonstrates earliest historicizing reception rather than textual variant.
  v2: בִּיהוּדָה...בְּיִשְׂרָאֵל
      Judah//Israel merism = whole covenant people; chiastic arrangement verb-location // adjective-location(reversed) creates envelope structure within single verse.
  v6: לֹא־מָצְאוּ...יְדֵיהֶם
      Idiom for utter incapacity/helplessness; 'hands' = agency/instrument motif, victory achieved without human counter-action.
  v11: חֲמַת...חֵמֹת
      Polyptoton — same root ח.מ.ה 'heat/wrath' in construct singular then plural — deliberate repetition/intensification device.

=== S5 max: 10 new phrase targets ===
  v1: מִזְמ֖וֹר...שִֽׁיר
      Double genre-label mizmor+shir (cf. Pss 65-68,83,87-88,92,108); Asaph-attribution לְאָסָף interposed BETWEEN the two genre terms — same interrupted pattern in Ps 65:1, likely fixed convention not unique disruption
  v1: πρός τὸν Ἀσσύριον
      LXX-only plus, no MT counterpart; earliest datable link of Ps 76 to Sennacherib's 701 BCE campaign; possibly reflects variant Vorlage, exegetical gloss, or association via shared lion-den/prey vocabulary w/ Nah 2:12-13's Assyria oracle (see vv.3,5)
  v2: בִּיהוּדָ֣ה...בְּ֝יִשְׂרָאֵ֗ל
      Judah named BEFORE Israel (reverses common order) — southern/Zion-centric perspective privileging Judah as revelation-site, broadened to whole-nation merism
  v3: וַיְהִ֣י
      Vav-consecutive narrative form (wayyiqtol) — unusual amid hymn's timeless perfects/participles; implies specific 'coming-to-pass,' historicizing tone (echo of Davidic capture of Jebusite Zion, 2 Sam 5, as election-event)
  v4: סֶֽלָה
      First of two Selahs (v.4, v.10), structural marker closing opening victory-strophe (vv.2-4)
  v5: ὄρους αἰωνίου (LXX)
      Diverges from MT in BOTH lexeme ('prey' vs 'eternal') and number (pl. mountains vs sg. mountain) — possibly reflects Vorlage הַרְרֵי־עַד, or theological smoothing toward Zion-as-eternal-mountain doctrine (cf. Ps 48) over wild predator-terrain
  v6: וְלֹֽא־מָצְא֖וּ...יְדֵיהֶֽם
      Idiom for total helplessness, possibly unique phrasing — warriors cannot even locate own hands; contrast general 'hands grow feeble' idiom (Isa 13:7; Ezek 7:17, 21:12)
  v10: סֶֽלָה
      Closes second major unit (vv.5-10), paired w/ v.4 Selah (closing vv.2-4) — bipartite liturgical structure
  v11: ἐγκατάλειμμα ἐνθυμίου ἑορτάσει σοι (LXX)
      ἑορτάσει implies LXX read תַּחְגֹּר as root חגג (festival) not חגר (gird); ἐνθύμιος ('passionate/feeling,' from θυμός) softens חֵמָה from hostile 'wrath' toward generic intense feeling, defusing paradox of 'wrath that praises'
  v13: {פ}
      Masoretic petuchah (open paragraph) marker closing psalm — paratextual scribal sense of major structural closure

=== S5.5 high: 1 new phrase targets ===
  v1: ᾠδὴ πρὸς τὸν Ἀσσύριον
      LXX-only addition. Reads the psalm as Sennacherib commentary (2 Kgs 19:35). It shapes reception of v.4 and vv.6-7 as the destruction of the army.

=== S5.5 xhigh: 4 new phrase targets ===
  v1: ᾠδὴ πρὸς τὸν Ἀσσύριον
      LXX-only tag, a unique historicizing title linking the psalm to Sennacherib/701 (2 Kgs 19:35). Nahum echoes inside the psalm (v3 me'onah, v5 taref, v6 namu) lend it intertextual support.
  v3: ויהי
      Wayyiqtol 'and it became' — narrative of an event or settling, not mere state. Opens the verse as report-form, contrasting hymnic participles in v2. Possible echo of an enthronement/taking-up-residence moment.
  v4: סלה
      Selah 1 — closes first strophe (vv2-4). With Selah at v10, psalm divides 3 / 6 / 3 verses (2-4 | 5-10 | 11-13).
  v10: סלה
      Selah 2 closes central unit (vv5-10); psalm structure 3-6-3.
