And in the daily Jewish prayer book, the second paragraph of the *Aleinu* prayer (composed in third-century Babylonia for the Rosh Hashanah kingship service) turns verse 8 into the closing plea of every service, asking God to remove the false gods from the earth in order לְתַקֵּן עוֹלָם בְּמַלְכוּת שַׁקַּי, "to repair the world under the sovereignty of the Almighty":
